## What this space is for

This is a place for long-form notes: things I've figured out, opinions I've
changed my mind on, and the occasional deep dive. Everything here is written
by hand, start to finish. No drafts generated for me, no "polish this up"
passes. The point is to keep an honest record of how I actually think and
write.

Over time that record becomes useful in a second way: it's a reference for my
voice. When I hand a tool a pile of my own words, it has something real to
learn from instead of guessing.

## How posts are stored

Each post is a single Markdown file in `src/content/blog/`. That's the whole
storage layer — plain text, versioned in git, no database and no external
service to log into. A post is just frontmatter and prose:

```mdx
---
title: A good title
description: One sentence that sells the read.
date: 2026-07-01
tags:
  - engineering
---

Then you just write.
```

The frontmatter is validated at build time, so a missing title or a malformed
date fails loudly instead of shipping broken.

## Why it's built this way

A few things mattered more than anything else:

- **I own it.** The words sit in my repository, not someone else's dashboard.
- **It's durable.** Plain Markdown will still open in twenty years.
- **It's reviewable.** Every edit is a diff with full history.

That's the entire philosophy. Now, the writing.
